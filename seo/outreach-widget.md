# Widget outreach kit

Page to share: https://elcalendariolaboral.es/widget/ (pick comunidad or city → copy the code).
The copied code is an iframe plus a plain credit link under it, `Calendario laboral 2027 · elcalendariolaboral.es`.
That outside link is the one search engines credit to the host site. The link inside the iframe counts for the embed URL.
Best window: November to mid-January, when every gestoría, union and HR blog posts "calendario laboral 2027".

Rules: one personal email per site, only where the widget fills a real gap (no calendar, an outdated 2026 one, or a
PDF that never gets updated). No paid links and no link swaps. Don't ask for a particular anchor text: Google treats
keyword-rich widget links as link spam, so the credit stays branded.

## 15 target types

| # | Target | Where to find them | Why they would embed it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small and mid-size gestorías and asesorías laborales | Google "gestoría + city", local business directories | They post the calendar to their clients every year, usually as a PDF. A widget that updates itself saves that job. |
| 2 | Colegios de Graduados Sociales (one per province) | cgsn.es lists every provincial colegio | Their members field holiday questions. A news or resources page with the calendar for their province fits them. |
| 3 | Colegios de Economistas and Gestores Administrativos (provincial) | Their web "recursos" or "circulares" sections | Same audience as gestorías. They often only link the BOE PDF, which has no local holidays and no weekdays. |
| 4 | Local and provincial pages of CCOO, UGT, CSIF, USO, CGT | Federation and province subsites, sector blogs | Every year they publish "calendario laboral 2027 de la provincia". The city version of the widget gives them the 2 local holidays too. |
| 5 | Comités de empresa and union branch blogs (Blogger, WordPress) | Search "comité de empresa" + "calendario laboral" | They are hand-made sites that copy the calendar as an image. An iframe is easier and stays correct. |
| 6 | HR and payroll blogs (RRHH, nóminas, software vendors' blogs) | Factorial, Sesame, Personio-type blogs, independent HR consultants | Their posts rank for "calendario laboral 2027". An interactive block with all 19 comunidades adds value to the post. |
| 7 | Small ayuntamientos without their own calendar page | Municipal sites (often on the same CMS vendor per province) | Many only publish the 2 local holidays as a news item. The city or comunidad widget gives residents the whole year. |
| 8 | Mancomunidades and diputaciones provinciales (employment and development areas) | Agencias de desarrollo local, "empleo" sections | They serve businesses and job seekers in villages with no site of their own. |
| 9 | Cámaras de Comercio (provincial) | camara.es directory | They write circulars for SMEs about holidays and working hours. A widget fits their "información empresarial" pages. |
| 10 | Business associations (CEOE provincial branches, local merchants' and hospitality associations) | Federaciones de comercio, asociaciones de hostelería | Shop opening on holidays depends on the calendar. Their members ask every December. |
| 11 | Coworkings and business centres | Local coworking sites and blogs | Freelancers plan invoices and deliveries around holidays. It is an easy resource page for them. |
| 12 | AMPAs and school websites (for the escolar pages, plus the labour widget for working parents) | AMPA blogs, school WordPress sites | Parents juggle school days and work holidays. Pair the widget with the link to /calendario-escolar/<comunidad>/. |
| 13 | Local news sites and city blogs (hyperlocal) | "Noticias de <city>", digital local papers | They publish "festivos 2027 en <city>" articles that get traffic every year. An embedded city widget stays correct after the article goes out. |
| 14 | Autónomo and small-business associations (ATA, UPTA, local ones) | Their "guías" and news sections | Autónomos look up holidays for deadlines and working days. |
| 15 | Transport, logistics and shift-work companies' employee portals and blogs (also security, cleaning and care services) | Company blogs, sector associations | Shift planning depends on regional and local holidays across several comunidades. The picker covers all of them. |

Start with types 1, 4, 7 and 13. They publish the calendar every year anyway, so the widget replaces work they already do.

## Email template (Spanish, 4 lines)

> Asunto: Calendario laboral 2027 de {comunidad/ciudad} para vuestra web
>
> Hola, he visto que publicáis cada año el calendario laboral en {URL de su página}.
> Hemos hecho un widget gratuito con los festivos 2027 de {comunidad/ciudad} según el BOE y el boletín de la comunidad (y los locales donde ya están publicados). Se actualiza solo, no lleva anuncios ni cookies y se pega con una línea de código.
> Lo tenéis aquí, con vista previa: https://elcalendariolaboral.es/widget/#{clave}
> Si veis cualquier error en las fechas, decídmelo y lo corrijo. Gracias, César.

`{clave}` opens the picker on that place: `madrid`, `galicia-vigo`, `canarias-tenerife`, `cataluna-barcelona`…
(the slug of the comunidad, or `<comunidad>-<ciudad>` / `<comunidad>-<isla>`).
