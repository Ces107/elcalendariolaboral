## Findings, by priority

1. **Scaled, templated city pages in four languages (critical).** 620 city URLs; the 465 ca/gl/eu copies are the
   Spanish template machine-filled (untranslated "Fiesta local (1er festivo)", "Publicidad"), with 2 ad units each.
   The 155 Spanish ones differ only in name, 2 dates and the source line: a textbook "many similar pages" case
   (AdSense step 3, doorway wording "pages targeted at specific regions or cities").
2. **Ads on languages AdSense does not support (critical).** Galician and Basque are not AdSense languages; every gl/eu
   page carried the loader and 1–2 units. Catalan pages are translations without editorial text of their own.
3. **Ads on tool, listing and legal screens (high).** Vacation planner (20), PDF hub, festivos-locales hub, widget page,
   /semana/, /dias-habiles/, privacy and 404 had the loader, so Auto ads (on since Nov 2026) could fill them.
   "Valuable inventory" bans ads on navigation/tool screens and pages without publisher content.
4. **No trust pages (high).** No About/Contact/Legal; privacy named no owner; contact was a personal address only on
   the privacy page. Organization markup had no contact.
5. **Little original explanation (high).** Comunidad pages were tool + table + 4 FAQ lines; no answer line, no "what is
   specific here", no visible update date. Home had no explanation of how holidays are set. No /puentes/ page although
   the nav linked "Puentes".
6. **Structured data (medium).** `WebApplication` on the home without rating/review (invalid for the software rich
   result). FAQPage everywhere: harmless but gives no rich result since May 2026. ItemList, Dataset and
   BreadcrumbList are fine.
7. **Dates (medium).** No visible "Actualizado" on data pages; sitemap lastmod was the data date for all pages, never
   the content date.
8. **Core Web Vitals (medium).** Google Fonts CSS was render-blocking and cross-origin (2 extra connections before
   first paint). Ad slots already reserve 300 px (no CLS). HTML ~52–60 KB per page (half is the embedded data JSON,
   ~12 KB gzipped): fine.
9. **hreflang (low).** City pages declared alternates to the templated ca/gl/eu copies; once these are noindex,
   the alternates must go.
10. **Thin escolar pages (low, no ads).** País Vasco and Navarra school pages are short because the orders give little;
    they show the official data and carry no units. Keep.

## Fixes applied (6 Nov 2026), in this order

1. ca/gl/eu city pages: `noindex,follow`, no canonical/hreflang, no ads, out of the sitemap (still linked for users).
   Spanish city pages: no hreflang.
2. Ads only on Spanish pages with editorial content (`build.ADS_LANGS`, `head_html(ads=…)`). Removed (loader and
   units) from ca/gl/eu, the vacation planner, PDF hub, festivos-locales hub, widget page, /semana/, /dias-habiles/,
   privacy, legal pages and 404.
3. Trust pages: /sobre/, /contacto/, /aviso-legal/; privacy names the owner and uses the contact address; footer links
   on every page; Organization markup with email and publishingPrinciples.
4. Original content computed from the data only (`guia.py`): answer line + "Actualizado · Fuente" under every H1 of
   home, comunidades and cities; "Lo que cambia en 2027" on the home; "Lo propio de {comunidad} en 2027" on each
   comunidad; "Qué cambian los festivos locales de {ciudad}" on each city (effect of each local day, puentes and
   working days vs the comunidad, cities sharing the date, official source); new /puentes/ and
   /como-se-fijan-los-festivos/ guides.
5. WebApplication removed; WebPage with dateModified on the guides; FAQPage kept only where the FAQ is visible.
6. Sitemap lastmod = max(data date, `CONTENT_UPDATED`) only for pages whose text changed.
7. Self-hosted Public Sans (3 woff2 subsets, 18 KB each, preload 400), Google Fonts removed.

After: **305 URLs in the sitemap**, loader on 202 pages, manual units on 178, 647 noindex pages (465 city translations
+ 182 widget embeds).
