# SERP research: elcalendariolaboral.es (17 Nov 2026)

Method: about 25 web searches plus 20 page fetches, all read-only. **Caveat:** the search tool is a US-based engine, not google.es. The rankings below show who competes, but they are not exact Google ES positions. SERP features (People Also Ask, featured snippets, Top Stories) could not be observed, so every SERP-feature statement is **unverified**. Google Trends returned HTTP 429, so the seasonality findings rest on publication dates, not search volume.

## 2. Top 10 actions (ranked by expected traffic ÷ effort)

1. **Get every confirmed 2027 local date onto town pages now, with the dates in the title.** The Mataró title format ("Calendario laboral Mataró 2027: festivos 17 may y 27 jul") is what already earns clicks.
   *Evidence:* the press has already reported dates our pages could carry. Each needs a check against the source before publishing:
   - Torrejón: 21–22 Jun (vivirediciones, 19 Oct 2026)
   - Sevilla: 14 Apr and 27 May (andaluciainforma, 23 Jun 2026; links to the acta)
   - Valencia: 22 Jan and 5 Apr (valencia.es / eldebate, Jul 2026)
   - Málaga: 19 Aug and 8 Sep
   - León: 24 Jun and 5 Oct
   - Santiago: 6 May and 16 Aug
   - A Coruña: Carnaval and San Juan
   - Caspe: 29 Mar and 25 Jun
   - Selva: 3 May and 10 Aug (BOIB)
   - Getafe: 6 and 17 May, provisional (UC3M PDF)

   Mark each one "aprobado en pleno, pendiente de boletín" where that applies. Mataró already does this.
2. **Province hubs for "festivos locales 2027 provincia de X", ready for the day each regional or provincial bulletin publishes its consolidated list.**
   *Evidence:* El Español CLM published the full Toledo list (about 226 municipalities, from DOCM) on 26 Dec 2023, so these lists arrive late in the year. laboral-social has no 2027 provincial local PDFs for CyL or País Vasco. fedratecalc has none at all ("Fiestas locales" section explains, no list). Being first, with a sortable table (municipio, fecha 1, fecha 2, fuente), is the advantage.
3. **Fix the "Discovered – not indexed" backlog (2,293 URLs) with crawl priority, not more pages.** Use province hubs that link every municipality. Add a sitemap split by comunidad, with `lastmod` changed only when the data really changes. Link from high-traffic pages (Mataró, Illescas) to their neighbours with confirmed dates.
   *Evidence:* the Sabadell page (fetched) is mostly template: locals pending, most neighbours marked "pendiente", no FAQ. Pages like that are what Google deprioritises. Enrich the pending pages with the 2026 dates already shown, plus "when 2027 is expected and where it will be published", rather than noindexing them.
4. **Fix two homepage defects (minutes of work).** The "Próximo festivo" card shows a 2026 date (8 Dec 2026) on a 2027 page. The stats card says "3 puentes" while the table below lists 5.
   *Evidence:* fetched homepage, "Actualizado 7 nov 2026". Inconsistent numbers on a YMYL-ish date page undermine trust and snippets.
5. **Add the full BOE-annex table per comunidad to the homepage and comunidad pages,** with columns fecha / día / festividad / comunidades. Also add a visible "Fuentes" block and a "Revisado <date>" stamp.
   *Evidence:* the top non-news result fedratecalc has exactly this: an annex table, notes for Canarias and Cataluña, Ceuta and Melilla, 6 FAQs, sources and a "Revisado 4 nov 2026" stamp. Payfit only lists dates by month with no table. Our homepage has 9 national dates and 5 FAQs, but no per-comunidad table.
6. **Publish a "Puentes 2027 por comunidad (and by municipio)" data page and use it as the press-kit angle.**
   *Evidence:* the Selectra table of long weekends per comunidad (16 Oct 2026) and the Diario de León headline "cinco puentes de tres días" (14 Oct 2026) show the press runs this angle. Only we can compute puentes per municipality from 4,096 towns, e.g. "the towns with the most long weekends in 2027". Expect mentions more than links (see item 8).
7. **Catalan-language parity for Catalonia and Valencia towns.**
   *Evidence:* ara.cat (es/en/ca editions) and bop.diba.cat rank for the Barcelona and Mataró queries, and Mataró is our top page. Check that the `/ca/` pages exist for municipalities, carry hreflang, and use "festius locals 2027 <municipi>" titles. **Unverified** whether `/ca/` town pages exist; only `/ca/` and `ca.py` were seen in the repo.
8. **Pitch regional outlets that cite sources in the article body, offering per-province data the day it lands.**
   *Evidence:* of the articles fetched, andaluciainforma (official sources) and valenciabonita (comunica.gva.es) linked out in the body. Selectra linked only BOE and Guardia Civil. Diario de León, gasteizhoy, democrata, valencianews and cuestioneslaborales had **no** external body links. Target the outlets that link and offer exclusive tables (e.g. "los 100 municipios de Sevilla y sus festivos 2027").
9. **Widget outreach to business associations, gestorías and AMPAs.** The embed already ships a followed link to the comunidad page.
   *Evidence:* the fetched `/widget/` snippet is an iframe plus a `<p>` with a plain `<a href=".../madrid/">` and no `rel`. **Change the anchor to the brand ("elcalendariolaboral.es") or add `rel="nofollow"`.** Google's link-spam policy lists "keyword-rich, hidden or low-quality links embedded in widgets that are distributed across various sites" as spam. One concrete target is Cecot (see section 3).
10. **School-calendar long tail by province, before the Christmas break.** Target "vacaciones navidad 2026 <provincia>" and "calendario escolar 2026-2027 <provincia>".
    *Evidence:* Andalucía's Christmas dates differ by province (23 vs 24 Dec start, per andaluciainforma and ayuda-social). CLM publishes provincial resolutions as PDFs (Albacete, Ciudad Real, Toledo). Unqualified queries are lost to Mexico. Demand at province level is **unverified**.

## 6. What NOT to do

- No paid links, link exchanges, PBNs, or mass directory or comment submissions.
- No keyword-rich or hidden links in the widget (Google link-spam policy). Use a brand anchor or nofollow.
- Don't chase `calendario laboral 2027`, `festivos 2027`, or unqualified `puentes 2027` / `calendario escolar 2026-2027` / `vacaciones navidad 2026`. Those are big domains or the wrong country.
- Don't generate more thin pages while 2,293 sit unindexed. Enrich and interlink the existing ones instead.
- Don't publish a town's 2027 dates as "oficial" from a single unconfirmed source; competitors already disagree on counts (8, 9 or 10 national holidays). Label the status (pleno / boletín) and cite the source.
- Don't self-link on Wikipedia. Don't bulk "Request indexing". Don't bump `lastmod` without a real data change.
