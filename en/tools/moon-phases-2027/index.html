Full moons in 2027

2027 has 12 full moons, one per month, starting with the Wolf Moon on 22 January and ending with the Cold Moon on 13 December. There is no blue moon this year: the last one was on 31 May 2026 and the next falls on 31 December 2028. A phase is a single instant for the whole planet, so the date can change with the time zone: the June full moon happens at 00:44 UTC on 19 June, which is still 18 June at 20:44 in New York.

Where the moon names come from

The monthly names (Wolf, Snow, Worm, Pink, Flower, Strawberry, Buck, Sturgeon, Corn, Hunter's, Beaver, Cold) are the ones popularised by North American almanacs, drawn from Algonquian and colonial traditions. Two of them move with the equinox rather than the calendar month: the Harvest Moon is the full moon closest to the September equinox, and the Hunter's Moon is the one after it. In 2027 the Harvest Moon falls on 15 September, a week before the equinox, so October gets the Hunter's Moon. A Blue Moon here means the second full moon in a calendar month, the most common modern use.

Other cultures name them differently, and supermoons (a full moon near perigee) are not marked, since the term has no agreed threshold.

How the phases are calculated

New moon is the moment the Moon and the Sun share the same ecliptic longitude as seen from Earth; first quarter, full moon and last quarter are 90, 180 and 270 degrees later. The calendar uses the series in chapter 49 of Jean Meeus' «Astronomical Algorithms»: the mean phase plus more than 40 periodic terms for the eccentricity of both orbits, the Moon's inclination and the pull of the planets, then the gap between dynamical time and clock time. Against the US Naval Observatory list for 2027, every one of the 49 phases agrees to within a minute.

Between phases, each day's moon is drawn by interpolating the phase angle at noon, so the lit percentage shown on hover is approximate (one or two percent). From one new moon to the next takes 29.53 days on average, but single lunar months vary by several hours because the Moon's orbit is elliptical.

Frequently asked questions

How many full moons are there in 2027?

Twelve: 22 January, 20 February, 22 March, 20 April, 20 May, 19 June, 18 July, 17 August, 15 September, 15 October, 14 November and 13 December (UTC dates).

When is the next blue moon?

On 31 December 2028, the second full moon of that month. The previous one was on 31 May 2026.

Is the waxing moon lit on the right or the left?

In the northern hemisphere a waxing moon is lit on the right and a waning moon on the left. South of the equator it is the other way round, and near the equator the crescent looks more like a smile.

Why does my calendar show a different date for the full moon?

Because the phase is one instant worldwide. A full moon at 00:44 UTC on 19 June 2027 is still the evening of 18 June in the Americas. Set the time zone above to match where you are.

What is the Harvest Moon in 2027?

The full moon of 15 September 2027, the closest to the September equinox. The full moon of 15 October is the Hunter's Moon.
