KDP royalty calculator

KDP rates as of November 2026.

Format
MB

What you earn per copy

A 6 × 9 in paperback with 120 black-and-white pages, listed at $9.99 on Amazon.com, costs $2.44 to print ($1.00 fixed + 120 × $0.012). KDP pays 60% of the list price minus printing: $3.55 a copy. Drop the price one cent to $9.98 and the rate falls to 50%, leaving $2.55, which is $1.00 less per sale. At 300 pages and $14.99 printing is $4.60 and the royalty $4.39. On Amazon.co.uk the same book at £7.99 earns £2.74.

Color is where margins vanish: an 8.5 × 11 in premium-color book with 100 pages costs $9.00 to print and at $19.99 earns $2.99. Check the "Minimum price" line before you set a price: KDP will not publish below it.

The 60% threshold

For paperbacks, KDP pays 60% only when the list price is at least $9.99 on Amazon.com, £7.99 on Amazon.co.uk, €9.99 on the euro stores, C$13.99 / A$13.99 in Canada and Australia, ¥1,000 in Japan, 40 zł in Poland and 110 kr in Sweden. Below that it pays 50%. The formula is the same either way: royalty = rate × list price − printing. In the UK and EU the list price you enter is before VAT; Amazon adds the local VAT on books on top (zero in the UK, 7% in Germany, 4% in Spain).

Expanded Distribution (US bookstores and libraries) pays 40% of the Amazon.com list price minus printing. For thin, cheap books it is often zero or negative, and KDP then asks for a higher price.

How printing cost works

On Amazon.com a black-and-white book of 24 to 110 pages costs a flat $2.30 ($2.84 for large trim); above 110 pages, $1.00 plus $0.012 per page ($0.017 large). Premium color is $3.60 up to 40 pages, then $1.00 + $0.065 per page ($0.08 large); standard color, 72 to 600 pages, $1.00 + $0.0255 per page ($0.0402 large). In the UK: £0.85 + £0.010 per page in black and white. A trim counts as large when it is wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in, so 6.14 × 9.21 already is. Cream and white paper cost the same.

eBooks: 70% or 35%

A $4.99 eBook on Amazon.com at 70% pays 70% of the price minus a delivery fee of $0.15 per MB: with a 2 MB file that is $3.28; at 35% it would be $1.75, with no delivery fee. The 70% option applies only between $2.99 and $12.99 (£1.77–£12.99 in the UK, €2.69–€12.99 in euro stores), and the eBook must be at least 20% cheaper than the print edition. In the UK, EU and Australia the price you enter includes VAT or GST, and KDP removes it first. Image-heavy books get heavy: from about 10 MB the 35% rate can pay more.

Frequently asked questions

How much does KDP pay for a paperback?

60% of the list price minus printing if the price is at least $9.99 (£7.99, €9.99), otherwise 50%. A 120-page black-and-white book at $9.99 earns about $3.55 on Amazon.com.

Why can't I price my paperback at $4.99?

KDP will not accept a price whose royalty does not cover printing. The minimum is printing ÷ 0.5; if that is above the 60% threshold, printing ÷ 0.6.

Does KDP's list price include VAT?

For paperbacks on UK and EU stores, no: Amazon adds VAT on top. For eBooks, yes: the price includes VAT and KDP removes it before calculating the royalty.

How much does it cost to print a book on KDP?

On Amazon.com, $1.00 plus $0.012 per page in black and white ($2.30 flat up to 110 pages). A 200-page book costs $3.40.

When does KDP pay royalties?

About 60 days after the end of the month of the sale, once you pass each marketplace's payment threshold.
