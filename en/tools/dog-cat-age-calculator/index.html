Dog and cat age in human years

Pet

How old is my dog or cat in human years?

The old «one dog year equals seven human years» rule does not hold up: dogs and cats grow up very fast and then age more slowly. The rule vets use is 15 human years for the first year and 24 by the second birthday. After that, each year adds about 4 human years for cats and small dogs (under 22 lb), 5 for medium dogs, 6 for large and 7 for giant breeds, because big dogs have shorter lives. A medium-sized dog of 5 is roughly 39 in human years; a 10-year-old cat, about 56.

For dogs the calculator also shows the «epigenetic clock» from a University of California San Diego study (Wang and colleagues, 2020), which matched DNA methylation changes in dogs and people: human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31. It runs older in the early years (a one-year-old dog is like a 31-year-old person) and younger in old age.

Examples

  • Kitten of 6 months: about 10 in human years, a cat in its early teens.
  • Cat of 14: 24 + 4 × 12 = 72, and a senior since 10 by the feline life-stage guidelines.
  • Yorkshire terrier of 10 (small): 24 + 4 × 8 = 56; on the epigenetic formula, 16 × ln(10) + 31 = 68.
  • Labrador of 8 (large): 24 + 6 × 6 = 60, a senior since 6.
  • Great Dane of 7 (giant): 24 + 7 × 5 = 59.

Sources and limits

The 15, 9 and then about 5 years rule is the one the American Kennel Club quotes from the American Veterinary Medical Association for a medium-sized dog; the size adjustment (4 to 7 years per year) is a simplified reading of the vets' chart by weight. The epigenetic formula is from Wang et al., Cell Systems (2020), built on 104 Labrador retrievers, so it fits very small or very large breeds less well and its authors do not apply it to puppies. Cat life stages follow the 2021 AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage Guidelines: kitten up to 1, young adult 1 to 6, mature adult 7 to 10 and senior from 10.

Use it to get a feel for when to move to twice-yearly senior check-ups or a senior diet; real health depends on breed, weight, neutering and lifestyle.

Frequently asked questions

Is one dog year really seven human years?

No. The first year is worth about 15 human years and the second about 9 more. After that it is 4 to 7 years per year depending on size.

How old is a 15-year-old cat in human years?

About 76: 24 at two, plus 4 for each year after that (24 + 4 × 13).

When is a dog considered senior?

Small and medium dogs at around 7; large and giant breeds earlier, at 5 or 6, because they age faster.

What is the logarithm formula for dog years?

It comes from a 2020 study that compared DNA methylation in Labradors and people: 16 × ln(age) + 31. It applies from age one; a 2-year-old dog comes out at 42 and a 12-year-old at 71.

How long do cats live?

Indoor cats usually reach 12 to 18 and many pass 20. Cats that roam outdoors live noticeably shorter lives on average.
