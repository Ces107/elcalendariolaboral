How a knockout bracket is built

A single-elimination bracket needs a power-of-two number of slots: 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64. When fewer players enter, the empty slots become byes and whoever is drawn against one goes straight through to round two. Ten players fill a 16-slot draw with 6 byes, so the first round has only 2 real matches and from the round of 16 on the draw is full. A knockout always takes players − 1 matches (9 for 10 players), plus one if you play off for third place.

List the names in ranking order and keep "Seeded": the top two land in opposite halves and can only meet in the final, seeds 3 and 4 go to opposite quarters, and the byes go to the highest seeds, exactly as in sanctioned events. "Random draw" shuffles the names first; the draw number lets you reproduce the same bracket later.

Where each seed goes

Placement follows the classic rule: in every round, seed s faces the seed that adds up with it to the draw size plus one. In a 16-player draw the first round reads 1 v 16, 8 v 9, 5 v 12, 4 v 13, 3 v 14, 6 v 11, 7 v 10 and 2 v 15 from top to bottom. If every favourite wins, the quarter-finals are 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 3 v 6 and 2 v 7, and the semi-finals 1 v 4 and 2 v 3. With 10 entries the top six seeds get byes and the only first-round matches are 8 v 9 and 7 v 10.

Recording results and sharing

On the day, tap the winner of each match and the name moves into the next round; tap again to undo. Changing a result clears the later matches that depended on it, so nobody advances by mistake. The results are saved inside the page link: "Copy link" and send it to the group, and everyone sees the live bracket. "Print or PDF" gives you a clean sheet to pin on the noticeboard or fill in by hand.

Frequently asked questions

How many byes in a 12-player bracket?

Four. The draw has 16 slots and 16 − 12 = 4, so the top four seeds go straight into the second round.

How many matches in a single-elimination tournament?

One fewer than the number of players, because every match knocks out exactly one: 15 matches for 16 players, 31 for 32.

Why are seeds 1 and 2 in opposite halves?

So the two favourites can only meet in the final. Tennis, padel, badminton and most federations place seeds this way.

Can I make a completely random bracket?

Yes, choose "Random draw". Names are shuffled before they go into the slots, and the byes stay spread out so two byes never meet.

Do I need a third-place match?

Only if you hand out prizes or medals to the top three. It is played between the two losing semi-finalists.
