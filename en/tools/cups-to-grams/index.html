How the conversion works

A cup measures volume, and every ingredient packs a different weight into the same volume: a cup of all-purpose flour weighs 125 g, a cup of sugar 200 g and a cup of honey 339 g. There is no single «cups to grams» factor. This converter uses the cup weight USDA FoodData Central publishes for each food (US cup of 236.6 ml, spooned and leveled) and scales it through millilitres to tablespoons, teaspoons, fluid ounces or metric cups.

Example: a chocolate chip cookie recipe calls for 2¼ cups of flour, 1 cup of butter, ¾ cup of packed brown sugar and 2 cups of chips. That is 281 g of flour (2.25 × 125), 227 g of butter, 165 g of brown sugar and 336 g of chocolate chips. Write the gram amounts on the recipe once and it will come out the same every time.

Why bakers weigh

Measuring dry ingredients by volume has a large error: flour settles in the bag, powdered sugar clumps and cocoa changes a lot once sifted. A few extra grams of flour are the difference between a moist cake and a dry one. If you have a kitchen scale, weigh; if not, spoon the flour into the cup without pressing and level it with a knife. Liquids are the exception: water and milk weigh almost exactly as many grams as their millilitres.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of flour?

About 125 g for all-purpose flour spooned into the cup and leveled, per USDA FoodData Central. Scooping straight from the bag packs it down and can push a cup past 150 g, which is why baking recipes that list grams turn out more consistently. Bread flour is about 137 g a cup, whole wheat 120 g.

How many grams in a cup of sugar?

200 g of granulated sugar, 220 g of brown sugar packed firmly, 120 g of powdered sugar straight from the box (100 g sifted).

How many grams is a cup of butter?

227 g, which is two US sticks (113.5 g each) or half a pound. One tablespoon of butter is 14.2 g.

Is a US cup the same as a metric cup?

No. A US customary cup is 236.6 ml (nutrition labels round it to 240 ml); Australia, Canada and New Zealand use a 250 ml metric cup, about 6 % bigger. A UK recipe in «cups» usually means the 250 ml metric cup too. Pick the unit your recipe was written in.

How many grams in a tablespoon?

It depends on the ingredient: roughly 8 g of flour, 12.5 g of sugar, 18 g of table salt, 13.5 g of oil and 21 g of honey per level US tablespoon (14.8 ml).
