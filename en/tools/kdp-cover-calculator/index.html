Full cover dimensions

A KDP paperback cover is one PDF holding back cover, spine and front cover, plus 0.125 in (3.2 mm) of bleed on the top, bottom and both outside edges. Width = bleed + back + spine + front + bleed; height = bleed + trim height + bleed. For a 6 × 9 in book with 120 pages on white paper the spine is 0.270 in (6.9 mm) and the full cover 12.520 × 9.25 in (318.0 × 234.9 mm). At 300 DPI that is a 3,756 × 2,775 px canvas.

With 200 pages of cream paper at 5.5 × 8.5 in the spine grows to 0.500 in and the cover to 11.750 × 8.75 in. An 8.5 × 11 in color notebook with only 24 pages has a 0.056 in spine: too thin for any text.

Spine width by paper

  • White paper, black ink: pages × 0.002252 in (0.0572 mm per page).
  • Cream paper, black ink: pages × 0.0025 in (0.0635 mm). Cream is thicker, so the same book gets a spine about 11% wider.
  • Standard color: pages × 0.002252 in (0.0572 mm).
  • Premium color: pages × 0.002347 in (0.0596 mm).

"Pages" means page sides, not sheets: a 120-page interior PDF is 60 sheets. If the page count changes after you design the cover, the spine changes and KDP rejects the file, so rebuild the cover from the final count.

Safe areas and spine text

KDP allows text on the spine only from 79 pages (its Cover Creator asks for 80), and that text needs at least 0.0625 in (1.6 mm) of space from each spine edge. On the front and back, keep important text 0.125 in (3.2 mm) inside the trim, the dashed line in the sketch: anything in the pink band is cut off. The bottom-right corner of the back cover is where KDP prints its barcode if you do not supply one, so keep it clear.

Export a single PDF with images at 300 DPI or more. Hardcovers use a different template with a wrap margin; this calculator is for paperbacks.

Frequently asked questions

How wide is the spine of a 100-page KDP book?

0.2252 in (5.7 mm) on white paper, 0.25 in (6.4 mm) on cream and 0.2347 in (6 mm) in premium color.

What size is the cover of a 6 × 9 book?

Width = 12.25 in plus the spine; height = 9.25 in. With 120 white pages, 12.52 × 9.25 in (318 × 235 mm).

How many pages do I need for spine text on KDP?

At least 79 (80 with Cover Creator). Below that, leave the spine plain or just colored.

How much bleed does a KDP cover need?

0.125 in (3.2 mm) on the top, bottom and both outside edges. There is no bleed at the spine.

What resolution should a KDP cover be?

300 DPI. Multiply width and height in inches by 300 to get the canvas size in pixels.
