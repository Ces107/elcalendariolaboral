How many rolls of wallpaper do I need

Wallpaper is worked out in drops (full-height strips), not square feet. A 12 × 10 ft room with one door has about 41.4 ft of wall to cover. A US double roll is 20.5 in wide and 33 ft long; with 8 ft walls and 4 in left for trimming, each drop is 8.33 ft, so 3 drops come out of each roll. You need 25 drops, which means 9 double rolls.

Dividing by area gives the wrong answer: those walls are about 330 sq ft and a double roll holds 56 sq ft, which suggests six rolls. But the last 8 ft of each roll is too short for another full drop. Offcuts do get used above doors and above and below windows, which is why windows are not subtracted. For a Euro roll (0.53 × 10.05 m) in a 4 × 3 m room with 2.5 m walls you need 25 drops at 3 per roll: 9 rolls.

Pattern repeat and match

With a pattern, each drop must start at the same point of the design as the one next to it, and that costs paper. The repeat is printed on the label. With a straight match the motif lines up at the same height across drops, so each drop is rounded up to a whole number of repeats. With an offset (drop or half-drop) match the motif steps down half a repeat on every other drop, which wastes on average another half repeat per drop.

In the same room with 9 ft walls, a 21 in repeat with an offset match means cutting drops of 11.38 ft: only 2 per roll, and 13 double rolls in total. Plain papers and random-match designs are calculated with a repeat of 0.

Roll sizes and batch numbers

In Europe the standard roll is set by EN 233: 5.326 m² with a nominal width of 500 to 530 mm, which is the familiar 0.53 × 10.05 m roll. In the US, wallpaper is priced per single roll but sold in double rolls of about 56 sq ft (20.5 in × 33 ft), and wide 27 in papers also exist. Check the label: some brands use slightly different lengths, so enter your own size with «Other».

Make sure every roll carries the same batch or lot number, since the shade can change between print runs. A spare roll costs little compared with not finding the same batch months later for a repair.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet does a roll of wallpaper cover?

A US double roll holds about 56 sq ft, but after trimming and matching expect 45 to 50 sq ft of wall. A Euro roll is 5.3 m², usually covering about 4 m².

How many rolls for an accent wall?

For a 12 ft wide, 8 ft high wall with a US double roll: 8 drops, 3 per roll, so 3 double rolls without a large repeat.

Do I subtract windows and doors?

Subtract doors and floor-to-ceiling openings. Leave windows in: the offcuts are used above and below them.

What is a half-drop match?

A pattern that steps down half a repeat from one drop to the next. It wastes more paper than a straight match, on average half a repeat per drop.

How much extra length do I allow per drop?

About 2 in at the top and 2 in at the bottom, 4 in per drop, to trim against uneven ceilings and baseboards.
