Roasting time calculator

How the time is worked out

Pick the roast and its weight. The calculator follows the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service timetables for a conventional oven. Turkey and chicken use weight brackets: a 14 lb unstuffed turkey sits at the top of the 12-14 lb row, 3 to 3¾ hours at 325 °F, so it estimates 3 h 45 min. Beef, pork and lamb use minutes per pound: a 5 lb standing rib roast at 23-25 min/lb takes 1 h 55 to 2 h 05.

Enter the time you want to eat and it tells you when the roast goes in, resting time included, and when to start checking with a thermometer. For a 16 lb stuffed turkey (4 to 4¼ hours) and dinner at 4:00 pm, it goes in at about 11:33 am; start probing around 3:00 pm.

Safe internal temperatures

Time only gets you close: ovens vary and the shape of the roast matters as much as the weight. Safety comes from the temperature in the center, read with a food thermometer:

  • Turkey, chicken and all poultry: 165 °F (74 °C) in the thigh, wing and thickest part of the breast; stuffing 165 °F too.
  • Beef, pork, lamb and veal steaks, chops and roasts: 145 °F (63 °C) plus at least 3 minutes of rest.
  • Ground meat: 160 °F (71 °C). These charts are not for meatloaf or burgers.
  • Fully cooked ham being reheated: 140 °F (60 °C).

The USDA suggests letting a turkey stand 20 minutes before removing the stuffing and carving. Ten to fifteen minutes' rest also keeps beef, pork and lamb juicier when you slice.

Thanksgiving turkey planner

  • 12 lb unstuffed: top of the 8-12 lb row, 2¾ to 3 hours at 325 °F (estimate 3 h). Thaw: 3 days in the fridge.
  • 16 lb stuffed: 4 to 4¼ hours (estimate 4 h 08). Thaw: 4 days.
  • 22 lb unstuffed: 4½ to 5 hours (estimate 4 h 45). Thaw: 5½ days, so it goes in the fridge on the Friday before.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I cook a turkey per pound?

At 325 °F, an unstuffed 12-14 lb turkey takes 3 to 3¾ hours and a 20-24 lb bird 4½ to 5 hours, according to the USDA chart. That works out to roughly 13 to 15 minutes per pound, falling as birds get bigger, which is why this calculator follows the chart instead of one fixed rate. A stuffed bird needs up to half an hour more.

What temperature should turkey be when done?

165 °F (74 °C) measured in the innermost part of the thigh and wing and the thickest part of the breast. If you stuff the bird, the center of the stuffing must also reach 165 °F.

How long does a turkey take to thaw?

In the refrigerator, about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. A 14 lb turkey needs three to four days, so start the weekend before Thanksgiving. Never thaw it on the counter.

What about prime rib, ham and pork loin?

Whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb and veal are safe at 145 °F (63 °C) with a 3-minute rest. A fully cooked ham only needs reheating to 140 °F (60 °C). Many cooks take prime rib out earlier for rare; 145 °F is the USDA's safe minimum, not a doneness target.

Does a convection oven change the time?

The USDA charts assume a conventional oven. Convection cooks faster; manufacturers usually suggest lowering the temperature by about 25 °F or checking earlier. Either way, the thermometer is the real test.
