How each code is built

Type the product and, for each variant, its values separated by commas. The generator makes one SKU per combination: "Basic tee" with three colors (Red, Blue, Black) and four sizes (S, M, L, XL) gives 12 codes, from BAST-RED-S to BAST-BLA-XL. Each value is cut to its first letters, with accents and symbols removed; later words add their initial ("Navy blue" → NAVB), and sizes or measures with digits (XL, 3XL, 11OZ) stay whole. If two values would produce the same code, the prefix grows until they differ, so no two SKUs are ever the same: with two letters, Blue and Black would both be BL, so they become BLU and BLA.

Below the table you get the list as CSV (SKU plus variants), ready to paste into a spreadsheet or a bulk import: Etsy, Shopify, WooCommerce and Amazon Seller Central all have a SKU field per variant.

Good SKU habits

  • General to specific: product, model, color, size. Sorted alphabetically, all variants of a product then sit together.
  • Short, no spaces: 8 to 16 characters fit every field and every warehouse label.
  • Never start with zero: Excel and Google Sheets turn 0012 into 12.
  • Avoid look-alikes: O and 0, I and 1 get mixed up when someone reads a label by hand.
  • Never reuse a SKU from a retired product, or your sales reports will merge the two.

A SKU is not a barcode

A SKU is internal: you make it up and it only means something in your shop and your stockroom. The barcode Amazon or a retailer asks for is a GTIN (UPC in North America, EAN-13 elsewhere), issued by GS1 and unique worldwide. Most sellers use both: the GTIN identifies the product to everyone else, the SKU helps you find it, count it and read sales by variant.

Frequently asked questions

What is a SKU?

A stock keeping unit: an internal code for each variant of a product (say, the red T-shirt in size M) used to track inventory and sales.

How do I create SKUs?

Join fixed abbreviations for product and variants in the same order, separated by hyphens: BAST-RED-M. This generator does it for every combination at once.

Do I need SKUs on Etsy or Amazon?

On Etsy they are optional; on Amazon Seller Central every offer has a seller SKU, and Amazon creates one if you leave it blank.

Is a SKU the same as a UPC or EAN?

No. UPC and EAN are GS1 barcodes, unique worldwide; a SKU is your own code and only works inside your business.

How long should a SKU be?

There is no standard, but 8 to 16 characters of letters, digits and hyphens works on every platform.
