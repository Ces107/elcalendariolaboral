Worked example: a phone photo

Most phones shoot 4032 × 3024 px (4:3). For an Etsy listing photo at 4:3 nothing is cropped: shrink it to 66% to get 2667 × 2000 and keep the file under 1 MB. For a Pinterest pin (2:3, portrait) you keep only a central strip of 2016 × 3024, throwing away half the picture, so shoot portrait from the start. For a YouTube thumbnail (16:9) you trim 378 px from the top and the same from the bottom; if the photo were only 1920 × 1080 you would have to double it to reach 3840 × 2160 and it would go soft, so start from a large image.

Keep the important part safe

Etsy shows the same photo cropped to square, portrait and landscape depending on the page, so leave space around the product and never run text to the edges (Etsy's image guide). On a YouTube banner only the central 1,546 × 423 px shows on every device: logo and text go there. Instagram's profile grid shows posts cropped to portrait, so keep the subject centred. For a Kindle eBook cover KDP recommends 1600 × 2560 px (KDP help), and A+ modules take JPG or PNG files up to 3 MB.

Frequently asked questions

What size should Etsy listing photos be?

At least 2,000 px wide and tall, with the first photo landscape or square. At 4:3, 2667 × 2000 px. Below 635 px Etsy ranks the listing lower.

What is the best Pinterest pin size?

1000 × 1500 px, a 2:3 ratio. Wider pins may be cropped in the feed.

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

16:9; YouTube recommends 3840 × 2160 px with a minimum width of 640 px. From a phone the file can be at most 2 MB.

What size is an Instagram story?

1080 × 1920 px, a 9:16 ratio, the same as reels.

What size is a KDP eBook cover?

1600 × 2560 px (width × height), front cover only.
