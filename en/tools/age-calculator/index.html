Age calculator

How your exact age is worked out

Someone born on 15 May 1990 is 36 years, 5 months and 28 days old on 12 November 2026: 36 whole years up to 15 May 2026, five whole months to 15 October, then 28 more days. That is 13,330 days lived, or 1,904 weeks and 2 days, and the day of birth was a Tuesday. The calculator counts whole years first, then whole months, then the days left over, the way people state an age out loud.

The age is worked out for today by default, but «On date» takes any other date: how old someone was on a past event, how old they will be on a trip or an exam, or a parent's age when a child was born. It also shows the next birthday, how many days are left and which weekday it falls on, plus the date of your next thousand-day milestone, a fun one to celebrate: 10,000 days arrives at about 27 years and 4 months.

Leap-day birthdays and legal age

People born on 29 February only get their exact birthday in leap years. In other years this calculator adds the year on 28 February, the last day of the month, which is how many legal systems count terms in years when the final month has no matching day. Others celebrate on 1 March; the age in days is the same either way.

For legal age, most countries count a birthday from the start of the day, not the hour of birth: someone born on 15 May 2009 turns 18 at midnight on 15 May 2027. If the person is under 18, the result also shows the date they come of age. Rules for school years, pensions or insurance often use a cut-off date instead, so check the date your form asks for.

Frequently asked questions

How many days old am I?

Enter your date of birth: «Days lived» counts every day from then to today, not counting the day you were born.

When will I be 10,000 days old?

At 27 years, 4 months and 15 to 18 days, depending on your birth date. The «You reach» row gives the exact date of your next thousand-day mark.

How old was I on a specific date?

Replace «On date» with that date. The result gives your age then in years, months and days.

How is age counted for someone born on 29 February?

In common years this calculator counts the birthday on 28 February. Some people and some rules use 1 March instead.

Why does the number of weeks not match years times 52?

A year is 52 weeks and one or two days, so the extra days add up: 36 years is about 1,878 weeks, not 1,872.
