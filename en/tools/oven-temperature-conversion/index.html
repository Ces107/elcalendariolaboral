Oven temperature conversion

In
Recipe oven

How the conversion works

Enter the recipe temperature and its scale. Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9; Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32. So 350 °F is 176.7 °C and 220 °C is 428 °F. Recipes never use exact figures: oven dials move in steps of 10 or 25 degrees and a 5-degree difference does not change a cake, which is why recipes round 177 °C to 180 °C and 392 °F to 400 °F. The calculator shows the exact value and the dial value.

A British recipe that says «gas mark 6» means 400 °F, about 200 °C conventional or 180 °C fan. The full table:

Fan, convection and conventional ovens

A fan circulates hot air, so food browns and cooks faster and the temperature comes down. UK and European oven makers advise 20 °C lower than a conventional (top and bottom heat) setting; American convection guidance is 25 °F lower, almost the same thing (about 14 °C). A recipe that already says «180 °C fan» is adjusted: in an oven without a fan that is 200 °C. Choose «Fan / convection» under «Recipe oven» and the calculator works it back to the conventional equivalent.

Ovens also drift from their dial, sometimes by 20 °F or more. An inexpensive oven thermometer tells you how far yours is off, which matters more than any conversion table.

Frequently asked questions

What is 350 °F in Celsius?

176.7 °C, usually written as 180 °C in European recipes (or 160 °C fan). It is gas mark 4 in UK recipes.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. 200 °C × 9 ÷ 5 = 360; + 32 = 392 °F, which recipes round to 400 °F. Going the other way, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9.

How much lower for a fan or convection oven?

UK and European recipes reduce by 20 °C for a fan oven (180 °C conventional becomes 160 °C fan). US guidance for convection is 25 °F lower (350 °F becomes 325 °F). If the recipe already says «fan», do not reduce it again.

What does gas mark 4 mean?

Gas marks are the dial numbers of British gas cookers. Gas 4 is 350 °F (about 180 °C). Each mark adds 25 °F, from gas 1 at 275 °F to gas 9 at 475 °F; below 1 come ½ (250 °F) and ¼ (225 °F).

What is a moderate or hot oven?

Old recipe terms: slow or cool is about 300-325 °F (150-160 °C), moderate 350 °F (180 °C), moderately hot 375-400 °F, hot 400-425 °F (200-220 °C) and very hot 450 °F (230 °C) and above.
