How much gravel do I need

Multiply length × width × depth in feet to get cubic feet, divide by 27 for cubic yards, then multiply by the weight of the material. A 20 × 10 ft path with 3 in of gravel is 50 ft³; with 5% spare, 52.5 ft³ or 1.94 cubic yards, about 2.62 tons. In 50 lb bags that is 105 bags, so for anything beyond a small bed, bulk delivery by the yard or ton is far cheaper.

Typical depths: 2 to 3 in of pea gravel or decorative stone over landscape fabric, 3 to 4 in for paths you walk on, 4 to 6 in of compacted crushed stone as a base for a patio or a shed, 2 to 3 in of mulch on flower beds and 4 to 6 in of topsoil for a new lawn. For the same 20 × 10 ft bed, 3 in of bark mulch is 52.5 ft³, about 27 bags of 2 ft³.

Weights and densities

Aggregates are sold by weight or by the yard, so the loose bulk density matters. Gravel and sand run at about 100 lb per cubic foot (1,600 kg/m³), roughly 1.35 short tons per cubic yard; building codes give ranges of 1,400 to 1,900 kg/m³ for sand and 1,500 to 2,000 kg/m³ for sand and gravel. Topsoil is lighter when dry, around 80 lb/ft³, and much heavier wet. Mulch and compost are light and sold by volume, which is why the calculator switches to bags of 2 ft³ (or 50 L) for them. If your supplier quotes another weight per yard, type it in.

Crushed stone base compacts: plate-compacting removes 15 to 20% of the loose volume, so add that under «Spare» for a compacted base.

Lawn: seed or sod

Seeding rates depend on the grass: about 3 to 4 lb per 1,000 sq ft for Kentucky bluegrass, 6 to 8 lb for tall fescue and perennial ryegrass mixes. The calculator uses 7 lb per 1,000 sq ft (about 35 g/m²), a common figure for a sun-and-shade mix; change it under More options to the rate on your seed bag. Sod is sold by the square foot or by the pallet (around 450 to 500 sq ft); order 5% extra for edges and lay it the day it arrives on prepared, watered soil.

Frequently asked questions

How many tons of gravel are in a cubic yard?

About 1.35 short tons (2,700 lb) of loose gravel, between 1.25 and 1.7 depending on stone size and moisture.

How many bags of gravel make a yard?

About 54 bags of 50 lb, or 54 bags of 0.5 ft³. A cubic yard is 27 ft³.

How deep should gravel be for a driveway?

4 to 6 in of compacted crushed stone base plus 2 to 3 in of surface gravel, for 6 to 9 in in total.

How much mulch do I need?

A 2 to 3 in layer: 100 sq ft at 3 in is 25 ft³, about 0.93 cubic yards or 13 bags of 2 ft³.

How much topsoil for a new lawn?

4 to 6 in. For 1,000 sq ft at 4 in, about 12.3 cubic yards.
