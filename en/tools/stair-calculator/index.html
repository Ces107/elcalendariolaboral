How many steps and what size

Divide the total rise by a comfortable riser of about 7 in (17.5 cm) and round to get the number of risers. Then set the tread with Blondel's rule, 2 × riser + tread ≈ 25 in (63 cm), the length of an average stride. For a 9 ft (108 in) floor-to-floor rise that gives 16 risers of 6.75 in and treads of 11.30 in. The top floor acts as the last step, so there are 15 treads and the stair runs 14.13 ft horizontally.

In metric, a 2.6 m rise in a UK house gives 15 risers of 17.3 cm and goings of 28.3 cm, a run of 3.97 m. If the space is tight, enter the tread you can fit or a number of steps under More options and the calculator checks whether the result still meets the code you selected.

What the codes require

The International Residential Code (IRC R311.7), used across most of the US, limits risers to 7¾ in and requires treads of at least 10 in, measured nosing to nosing; one flight may rise at most 151 in between floors or landings. Risers in a flight may not differ by more than ⅜ in. Many cities adopt local amendments, so check with your building department.

In England, Approved Document K sets for a private stair (inside a dwelling) a rise of 150 to 220 mm, a going of at least 220 mm, 2R + G between 550 and 700 mm and a pitch of no more than 42°. Spain's CTE, the other option, asks for 13 to 18.5 cm risers and 28 cm treads in general-use stairs.

Measuring correctly

Measure the rise from finished floor to finished floor. If flooring is still to be laid at the bottom or top, add or subtract its thickness: a small error is shared by all steps in theory, but in practice it lands on the first or last step, the classic trip hazard. The stringer length shown is the diagonal from the bottom to the top tread, a starting point for cutting stock; add the extra the stringer needs at each end.

Frequently asked questions

What is the ideal rise and run for stairs?

A rise of about 7 in (17.5 cm) and a run of 11 in (28 cm). That satisfies Blondel's rule and every common code.

How many steps for a 9 foot ceiling?

With a 9 ft ceiling and a typical floor structure the rise is about 108 to 110 in: 15 or 16 risers of roughly 7 in.

What is the maximum riser height under the IRC?

7¾ in (196 mm), with treads of at least 10 in (254 mm). A flight can rise at most 151 in before a landing is required.

What is the 2R + G rule?

Twice the rise plus the going should fall between 550 and 700 mm in UK regulations, and close to 630 mm for a comfortable stair.

How much floor space does a staircase take?

The number of treads times the tread depth: 15 treads of 10.5 in take about 13 ft, plus a landing at the bottom.
